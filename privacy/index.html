Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-15

QuantumFinAI is operated by Youssef Nassiri in Morocco, who is responsible for the site's personal data processing. Contact youssef@quantumfinai.me for access, correction, deletion, privacy choices or a security concern. This notice describes the rewards service as it works today; it is not a claim of regulatory certification.

Information we collect and why

  • Account information: your name, email address, account ID and authentication information, to create and manage your account and send account or verification messages. Passwords are processed by the authentication service, not displayed to site administrators.
  • Reward activity: offers you start, partner and transaction identifiers, available offer names, timestamps, completion status, points, reversals and account review records. We receive completion and reversal information from partner networks to keep an accurate balance and investigate missing credits or misuse.
  • Security information: IP addresses, hashed IP and device identifiers, browser/platform details, screen size, language, timezone, hardware/GPU characteristics and fraud-risk results. These help detect duplicate devices, automation, VPNs, proxies and suspicious activity. Hashes and account IDs may still be personal data.
  • Support information: the messages and evidence you choose to send us to answer questions and handle requests. Do not send passwords, full payment card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Cookies, local storage and human checks

The site uses browser local storage to keep your signed-in session, a device identifier for fraud prevention and your sound preference. Cloudflare may use cookies and browser signals for site security and the Turnstile human check at signup. Clearing storage signs you out and resets saved preferences. Security checks are part of operating the account safely. We do not add separate marketing consent through the signup checkbox.

Who receives information

GitHub Pages hosts the website; Cloudflare provides network security and Turnstile; Supabase provides authentication, database and server functions; Zoho handles site email. IP reputation checks may send an IP address to Scamalytics or ProxyCheck when those services are used. The fraud-monitoring code includes a Google Gemini analysis function; security facts may be processed by Google if AI analysis is enabled.

When you choose an offerwall or survey partner, the launch may include your account/SubID, IP address, country or a hashed identifier needed for tracking. The partner also receives information directly from your browser and any answers or details you submit on its service. Active integrations include CPX Research, MobiVortex/VortexWall and Wannads. Other partners are identified in the dashboard when available. Their privacy policies and consent choices apply to their own collection. Check them before participating, especially before giving survey answers or sensitive information. A coming-soon logo alone does not launch a network.

Purposes and legal bases

Where GDPR or similar law applies, account administration and recording the rewards you request are processed to perform the service contract. Necessary fraud prevention, security and support are based on legitimate interests in protecting users and the service, subject to your rights. Legal duties may require some record keeping. Where consent is required for a partner's optional tracking, survey processing or other optional use, that consent must be obtained separately and can be withdrawn. Acknowledging this notice at signup is not a waiver of privacy rights or blanket consent to every third party.

Retention and international processing

Account data is kept while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. After closure or a deletion request, we assess what can be deleted and what must be retained for unresolved reward disputes, fraud investigations or legal duties. Those needs, rather than an invented fixed period, determine retention; ask us for details for your account. Providers may retain backups or security logs under their own retention rules.

The operator is in Morocco and service providers and networks may process information in other countries, including the United States and Europe. Their locations and transfer safeguards depend on the provider and service. Contact us to ask about the arrangements that apply to your data. We do not claim that every international transfer has been independently legally verified.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you can ask for access to or a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection to processing, including legitimate-interest processing. You can withdraw consent where we rely on it. You may complain to your local data protection authority. Contact support for a human review of a security flag or account hold. We may need proportionate identity verification before disclosing or deleting account data, and some requests have legal exceptions.

California privacy choices

Where the CCPA/CPRA applies, California residents may request the categories and specific information collected, its sources, purposes and recipients, correction or deletion, and opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. They may also request limits on uses of sensitive information where applicable. We do not sell account records for money. Partner tracking may have its own sale/sharing implications; this notice does not declare that all third-party tracking is exempt.

To exercise these choices, email youssef@quantumfinai.me with the request, including "Do not sell or share" if relevant. You will not be treated unfairly for exercising your rights. Avoid launching partner walls until any requested restriction is confirmed. A Global Privacy Control signal is not yet automatically applied across partner integrations; contact us and use each partner's privacy controls. We cannot describe the site as fully CCPA compliant until that behavior and provider arrangements have been checked.

Children, updates and contact

Do not use offers unless you meet their age and eligibility requirements and can lawfully use the service. If you believe a child submitted information without required authorization, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where required.
