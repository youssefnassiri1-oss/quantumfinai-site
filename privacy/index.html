Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-16 · Technical update 18:00 UTC

QuantumFinAI is operated by Youssef Nassiri in Morocco, who is responsible for the site's personal data processing. Contact youssef@quantumfinai.me for access, correction, deletion, privacy choices or a security concern. This notice describes the rewards service as it works today; it is not a claim of regulatory certification.

Information we collect and why

  • Account information: your name, email address, account ID and authentication information, to create and manage your account and send account or verification messages. Passwords are processed by the authentication service, not displayed to site administrators.
  • Reward activity: offers you start, partner and transaction identifiers, available offer names, timestamps, completion status, points, reversals and account review records. We receive completion and reversal information from partner networks to keep an accurate balance and investigate missing credits or misuse.
  • Security information: IP addresses, hashed IP and device identifiers, fraud-risk results and, only if you opt in, device and browser fingerprint signals (a random device identifier, canvas, graphics-card and audio fingerprints, screen, timezone and language details, automation indicators, and basic mouse, touch and scroll behaviour). These help detect duplicate devices, multiple accounts, automation, VPNs, proxies and suspicious activity. Client fingerprint collection runs only after you opt in to device fingerprinting in Privacy choices, is skipped entirely when your browser sends Global Privacy Control, and the fraud verdict is always computed on our server, never in your browser. Hashes and account IDs may still be personal data.
  • Support information: the messages and evidence you choose to send us to answer questions and handle requests. Do not send passwords, full payment card details or unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Browser notifications (optional): if you turn on browser alerts in Profile, we store your browser's push address and its encryption keys with your account, only to send you a notification when an offer is credited. They are shared with your browser vendor's push service to deliver the message, are removed when you turn alerts off or delete your account, and are never used for marketing.

Cookies, local storage and human checks

The site uses browser local storage to keep your signed-in session, privacy choices and your sound preference. A random device identifier and the fingerprint signals described above are optional and are not created until you choose device fingerprinting in Privacy choices. You can reject optional processing as easily as accepting it. Opening a partner requires a separate affirmative choice for that provider; cancelling does not load it. Privacy choices withdraws partner permissions, closes the active wall and removes the local device identifier. Cloudflare may use cookies and browser signals for site security and the Turnstile human check at signup. Clearing storage signs you out and resets saved preferences. Necessary server-side abuse checks are part of operating the account safely. Automated risk flags can place an account under review or block offers; they do not eliminate the right to ask support for human review. The operator must review contested decisions. We do not add separate marketing consent through the signup checkbox.

Who receives information

GitHub Pages hosts the website; Cloudflare provides network security and Turnstile; Supabase provides authentication, database and server functions; Zoho handles site email. IP reputation checks may send an IP address to Scamalytics or ProxyCheck when those services are used. The fraud-monitoring code includes a Google Gemini analysis function; security facts may be processed by Google if AI analysis is enabled.

When you choose an offerwall or survey partner, the launch may include your account/SubID, IP address, country or a hashed identifier needed for tracking. The partner also receives information directly from your browser and any answers or details you submit on its service. Active integrations include CPX Research, MobiVortex/VortexWall and Wannads. Other partners are identified in the dashboard when available. Their privacy policies and consent choices apply to their own collection. Check them before participating, especially before giving survey answers or sensitive information. A coming-soon logo alone does not launch a network.

Purposes and legal bases

Where GDPR or similar law applies, account administration and recording the rewards you request are processed to perform the service contract. Necessary fraud prevention, security and support are based on legitimate interests in protecting users and the service, subject to your rights. Legal duties may require some record keeping. Optional device identification and opening partner tracking are based on your separate opt-in choices, which can be withdrawn from Privacy choices. Partner-specific survey permissions remain the partner's responsibility. Consent records are stored against your account when signed in. Acknowledging this notice at signup is not a waiver of privacy rights or blanket consent to every third party.

Retention and international processing

Account data is kept while the account is active. A signed-in member can use Profile to export records, correct their display name or delete their account. Closure deletes the authentication account, device records and linked verification records; removes claim notes, payout destinations and raw callbacks; and clears the account link from reward accounting rows rather than destroying their financial totals. Pending withdrawals must first be resolved with support. Transaction IDs and partner records may remain personal data when a partner can relink them, so de-linking is not a claim of irreversible anonymisation. The operator must review any continued retention for disputes or legal duties. Daily database cleanup deletes expired verification tokens after one day, IP-check cache after 30 days, client fingerprint reports, inactive device records and rejected callbacks after 90 days, resolved fraud flags and audit snapshots after 180 days, and raw callback payloads after 90 days. Active disputes and unresolved fraud flags require individual review; provider backups and service logs follow provider schedules.

The operator is in Morocco and service providers and networks may process information in other countries, including the United States and Europe. Providers may act as processors or independent controllers depending on the service, and offer partners generally have their own processing roles. Their locations and transfer safeguards depend on the provider and service. Applicable DPAs, subprocessor lists, adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses and transfer-risk assessments must be checked before a transfer is treated as legally covered. Contact us to ask about the arrangements that apply to your data. We do not claim that every international transfer has been independently legally verified.

Your privacy rights

Use Profile for a JSON export, name correction and account deletion. Email correction, restriction, objection and records outside the self-service export (including support correspondence and provider logs) are handled through support. Requests are normally answered within one month, with lawful extensions explained. Depending on the law that applies to you, you can ask for access to or a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection to processing, including legitimate-interest processing. You can withdraw consent where we rely on it. You may complain to your local data protection authority. Contact support for a human review of a security flag or account hold. We may need proportionate identity verification before disclosing or deleting account data, and some requests have legal exceptions.

California privacy choices

Where the CCPA/CPRA applies, California residents may request the categories and specific information collected, its sources, purposes and recipients, correction or deletion, and opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. They may also request limits on uses of sensitive information where applicable. We do not sell account records for money. Partner tracking may have its own sale/sharing implications; this notice does not declare that all third-party tracking is exempt.

To exercise these choices, email youssef@quantumfinai.me with the request, including "Do not sell or share" if relevant. You will not be treated unfairly for exercising your rights. Avoid launching partner walls until any requested restriction is confirmed. The website honours navigator.globalPrivacyControl: when on, optional device identifiers and all new partner launches are blocked, even if you previously opted in. Privacy choices also lets you reject optional processing. This does not remotely erase data already held by a partner; contact the partner or us to exercise those rights. Full provider-side sale/sharing classification and contract review remain necessary.
