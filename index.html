How offers are verified

Every offer goes through the same checks before any points are added to a balance.

1

One claim per offer

Each account can start an offer once. Repeat claims are refused by the database itself.

2

Time and volume limits

Offers can set a minimum completion time and a total cap. Rapid-fire activity is rate limited and flagged.

3

Duplicate account checks

Accounts that share a device or a payout destination are flagged and moved to manual review.

4

Confirmed completions

Network confirmations carry a unique transaction ID. A repeated ID is ignored, so nothing is credited twice.

5

Manual review queue

Claims and withdrawal requests are reviewed by a person. Approved claims can be reversed if a network cancels them.

6

Full audit log

Every change to offers, claims, flags and requests is recorded with who made it and when.

FAQ

Can I withdraw money today?

You can submit a withdrawal request once you have enough points. Each request is reviewed before approval and you can follow its status in your account.

Are the listed networks partners?

No. The networks listed on this site are independent companies. We are not affiliated with them and no partnership is in place yet.

How do you prevent abuse?

One claim per offer per account, minimum completion times, rate limits, duplicate-account checks, a manual review queue and an audit log. Accounts can be placed under review or blocked.

What data do you keep?

Your name and email, for your account only. It is never sold or shared. We never ask for banking details.
